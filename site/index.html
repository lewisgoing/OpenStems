OS/01stem separator

drop a song in. drag stems out.

A free, open source desktop app for Mac and Windows, made for producers and simple enough for anyone. Drop in files, folders or a link, and each song comes back as stems you can drag into your DAW, with its tempo and key detected. It runs on your own GPU, with no cloud and no account.

download 0.4.0 source on github

01 how it works

How it works

  1. dropDrop files, folders or a link. Pick a recipe, a quality and a format.
  2. separateSongs run one at a time on your GPU, and each shows its progress.
  3. drag outListen with solo and mute, then drag one stem, all of them or a mix into your DAW.

02

Every stem

The every-stem recipe runs four models one after another and gives you 13 stems. Add them back together and you get the original song.

13 files, each sample in exactly one

e.01

No overlap between stems

No sound is in two files. There is no separate vocals or drums file, because their parts already add up to them. On a test song, the 13 stems summed to within −144.9 dB of the original.

e.02

Leftovers get their own stem

DrumSep's six pieces miss the room sound, bleed and hand percussion. That remainder goes into "other drums". On two test songs it was 21.6 and 25.8 dB quieter than the drums. Backing vocals are made the same way, as the vocals minus the lead.

e.03

Drag a whole group

Drag Drums and you get its seven parts mixed into one file. Leave the kick out and the file is named "drums no kick". Guitar, piano and other group together as Melody.

    t.01

    Fix the grid

    If the detected tempo is wrong, halve it, double it, tap it or type it. For an exact tempo, drag bar 1 onto the first downbeat and a later bar line onto its beat. A metronome click lets you check it by ear.

    t.02

    Several stems at once

    Pick more than one stem and each is drawn in its own color, so you can see where the drums drop out or the vocals come in. They export as one mix.

    t.03

    Separate only the part

    A setting lets you separate only the bars you selected on a song that is still waiting in the queue. That is faster, and the stems are as long as the selection.

    04

    What it does

    f.01

    Preview before you export

    Each stem gets a waveform with solo and mute, and they all play in sync. Tempo and key are detected per song.

    f.02

    Split drums into the kit

    The drum-kit recipe pulls out the drums, then splits them into kick, snare, toms, hi-hat, ride and crash.

    f.03

    Export a mix

    Mute or solo stems and drag out what you hear as a single file, for example the song without vocals.

    f.04

    Fix tempo and key

    If detection got the tempo or key wrong, type the right one. File names and the tempo written into the WAVs update to match.

    f.05

    Tempo sync for FL Studio

    With tempo sync on, WAV stems carry the detected tempo. FL Studio stretches clips up to about a minute long to the project tempo by itself. For a full stem, use Fit to tempo in the clip menu.

    f.06

    Separate from a link

    Paste a link and the audio is downloaded and queued like a file. The tray window (menu bar on a Mac) takes links and files too.

    Will it run?

    machine

    memory, gb

    Pick your machine.

    Main recipes
    full-song, drum-kit, melody, drumless
    Larger vocal models
    every-stem, vocals-instrumental, acapella-clean, full-song-best-vocals
    Cleanup
    dereverb, denoise
    53 stems
    mega-53, experimental

    "Too large" is an estimate and does not stop you. The app tries the GPU first and moves a model to the processor if memory runs out.

    Download 0.4.0

    p.01

    Windows

    nvidia · amd preview

    download for windows .exe
    • NVIDIA GTX 16 series, RTX 20 series and later. Built and tested on an RTX 5070 Ti. GTX 900 and 10 series get a separate build that has not been tested.
    • AMD Radeon a preview. A user has reported it working, but the project has not tested it. It needs a card AMD's PyTorch for Windows supports (RX 9070, RX 7900 XTX and others).
    • First launch sets up Python and PyTorch for your card, about 3 GB, then the recommended models, 3.4 GB.
    • SmartScreen warns once, because the installer is not code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway. No administrator rights needed.

    p.02

    macOS

    apple silicon preview

    download for mac .dmg
    • Apple Silicon M1 or later, macOS 14 or later. Intel Macs are not supported.
    • Status preview, tested on one M1 Max so far. Reports from other Macs are welcome.
    • First launch sets up Python and PyTorch, about 350 MB, then the recommended models.
    Opening it the first time

    The app is signed with the project's own certificate, not an Apple Developer ID, so macOS will not open it on a double click.

    1. Double-click OpenStems. macOS says it could not verify the app. Click Done.
    2. Open System Settings and click Privacy & Security.
    3. Under Security it says OpenStems was blocked. Click Open Anyway. The button stays there for about an hour after step 1.
    4. Confirm, and enter your login password if asked.

    If macOS says it "is damaged", move it to Applications and run this in Terminal:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/OpenStems.app
